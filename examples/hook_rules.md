# Hook rules

Pass this file with `--hook-rules examples/hook_rules.md`. Everything here is added
to Claude's brief when it picks and scores clips. Edit it to match your niche and voice.

## Openings that work
- A specific number or money figure in the first sentence ("$76 trillion", "4 out of 5").
- A confession or admission ("I quit every job that made me feel sick").
- Contradicting what most people believe ("Most people think X. It's actually Y.").
- A direct question the viewer wants answered.
- The start of a story with stakes ("One customer called me at midnight…").

## Openings to avoid
- Greetings, thank-yous, shout-outs or reading a username.
- Starting mid-thought: "and so", "that's why", "like I said", "anyway".
- Setup that takes more than one sentence before anything interesting happens.

## Payoffs
- End on the punchline, the number, the lesson or the twist, never trailing off.
- Prefer clips where the last line could be quoted on its own.

## Skip entirely
- Sponsor reads, product plugs and calls to subscribe.
- Segments that only make sense if you watched the previous 10 minutes.
